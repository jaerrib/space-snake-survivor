# Space Snake Survivor

## Overview

**Space Snake Survivor** is a modern twist on a classic idea.

Blast through nonstop waves of aliens and asteroids, growing stronger as you go...and also longer, which makes survival even trickier.

Each run takes you through ten walled sectors connected by laser gates. Every three minutes, the next gate opens, letting you move forward while unleashing tougher and more crowded enemy waves. Survive long enough to face the dreaded Neuropticlord and escape the gauntlet!

You start with a humble tongue attack, but you’re in luck because enemies drop XP when defeated. Leveling up increases your stats and unlocks new weapons. Unlike other survivor‑likes, there’s no buildcrafting here. Weapons follow a fixed, arcade‑style progression, so the challenge comes from movement, positioning, and dealing with your ever‑growing body. As your weapons level up, they’ll fire faster and hit harder to help you keep up with the chaos.

Walls are still dangerous; your own body isn’t. It’s safe to assume that you’ve got enough to deal with already.

## Controls

- WASD / arrow keys / D-pad / left joystick – move your snake / move between menu items
- Enter / Space / Bottom button (Xbox A, Sony Cross) – select menu options
- P / Start button – pause the game to take a break and check out your stats
- F2 / right shoulder button – toggle debug info, including the actual sector number rather than the sequence, FPS, and more
- F3 / left shoulder button – toggle scanlines
